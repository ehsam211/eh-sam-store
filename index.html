About EH SAM × EH Lite Store

EH SAM × EH Lite Store is owned and operated by Ejeh Samuel. It represents a blended ecosystem of tailored African fashion, contemporary apparel, graphic art, and functional lifestyle gadgets crafted to standard excellence.
