Privacy Policy

At EH SAM × EH Lite Store, we take your privacy seriously. This page explains how we collect and protect your information when you shop with us.

When you join our mailing list or request a custom design, we ask for your name and email. We only use this to send you updates and handle your requests. We do not store your payment details on our site; all payments are processed securely through trusted providers.
